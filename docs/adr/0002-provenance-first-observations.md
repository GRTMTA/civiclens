# Keep official records, possible matches, and observations separate

**Status: accepted.** CivicLens will never automatically attach a citizen observation to the top-ranked AI candidate, present a match heuristic as a probability, or turn observations into corruption or anomaly scores. The system will show possible record matches with understandable evidence, require resident confirmation, allow "None of these," and keep official source status separate from community observation status.

**Consequences:** Citizen-facing language uses Community observation and Possible record match; factual signals must retain source and date context; unverified observations need explicit labeling; an observation can remain nearby but unlinked.
