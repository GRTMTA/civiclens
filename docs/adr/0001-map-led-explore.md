# Map-led Explore with scan as the primary action

**Status: accepted.** CivicLens will use a map-led Explore experience rather than a conventional analytics dashboard, while keeping Scan Infrastructure as the dominant action for first-use activation. This makes documented projects local and explorable without forcing every resident into a scan flow, while preserving the scan-to-official-record journey as the product's clearest demonstration of value.

**Consequences:** The initial map must be framed with a clear task and usable empty/loading states; resident navigation stays small; dashboards, chart walls, heatmaps, saved projects, and scan history are not prototype requirements.
