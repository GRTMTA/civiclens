# 04 — Geometry Hierarchy and Compact Map Key

**What to build:** Map geometry communicates the difference between official records, reviewed estimates, and generic estimated areas without making estimates look like danger zones or official boundaries.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Connected Map and List Interaction.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Official geometry remains visually primary.
- [ ] Reviewed estimates and generic estimates have clearly distinct, lower-weight treatments.
- [ ] Estimated areas use lighter fills, thinner or dashed outlines, and appropriate zoom-level visibility without removing their functionality.
- [ ] The selected project treatment remains neutral and distinct from geometry provenance.
- [ ] The permanent legend becomes a discoverable `Map key` or `Legend` trigger with a compact popover.
- [ ] The map key explains status meaning, geometry provenance, and that estimated geometry is not an official project boundary.
